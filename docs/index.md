# :lucide-house: Scoutbook

Welcome to Scoutbook.org.uk — your ultimate, no-nonsense shortcut to the best Scout resources on the web! Built by a volunteer, for volunteers, this site completely skips the fluff, the out-of-date information, and the annoying ads. Instead, it’s just a clean, curated directory of basic info and direct links straight to the good stuff. 

While it's aimed at members of The Scout Association here in the UK, leaders from any other youth organisation are more than welcome to look around — you’ll find plenty of links and ideas that work across the board!

It’s a 100% independent labor of love with zero sponsorship, advertising, or financial backing — just one Scout volunteer trying to make everyone's life a little easier. 

Spot a great resource we missed? [Let us know](https://form.jotform.com/260734372907057) and help us keep the collective Scouting knowledgebase growing!

## The boring bit

This list is community-maintained. Anyone can suggest an entry using the link in the navigation — all submissions are reviewed before being added but we take no responsibility for the content of external websites. Inclusion on this site is not an indication of recommendation or endorsement.
